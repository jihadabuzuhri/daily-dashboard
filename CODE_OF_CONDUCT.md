# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

Everyone who takes part in this project — filing issues, reviewing code, opening pull requests, or
discussing ideas — is contributing their own time. We want that to be a good experience regardless
of experience level, background, identity, or how much you already know about the codebase.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good faith.
- Keep discussion focused on the technical substance, not on the person raising it.
- Give feedback on code, not on the contributor. "This will break when the list is empty" is
  useful; "this is sloppy" is not.
- Accept that a maintainer may decline a change. This is a small, deliberately focused project, and
  "good idea, out of scope" is a legitimate and final answer.
- Ask questions. Nobody is expected to already understand the persistence tiers or the drag-and-drop
  helper before their first contribution.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Discriminatory or demeaning comments about any personal characteristic.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussion, or deliberately derailing issues and reviews.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, commit messages, and code review — and
whenever someone is representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

If you see or experience behavior that violates this document, report it privately to the maintainer
([@jihadabuzuhri](https://github.com/jihadabuzuhri)), or through GitHub's
[report abuse](https://github.com/contact/report-abuse) form if you'd rather not contact the
maintainer directly.
