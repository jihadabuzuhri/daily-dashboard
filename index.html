        Install on iPhone

        iOS doesn't expose an automatic install button. To add Daily to your Home Screen:

        1. Tap the Share button at the bottom of Safari.
        2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
        3. Tap Add in the top-right corner.

        Make sure you're in Safari — other browsers on iOS don't support this.
